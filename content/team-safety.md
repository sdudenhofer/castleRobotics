Instilling a culture of safety is a value that every individual in the *FIRST* community must embrace as we pursue *FIRST*'s mission and vision. *FIRST* Robotics Competition has adopted safety as a core value and has established the framework for safety leadership in all aspects of the program.

*FIRST* believes that the teams who take the lead in developing safety programs and policies have a positive and lasting impact on each team member, mentor, their communities, and their present and future workplaces. *FIRST* recognizes the teams that demonstrate safety throughout their programs and are truly committed to developing and nurturing a safety culture.

**Castle Robotics integrates safety in all we do.** To accomplish this, all Castle Robotics students must complete the following homework.

## FIRST Safety Learning Portal

### Homework #1: Create a student account (new members only)

Do **not** do this if you created an account last season.

1. Go to the [FIRST Safety Learning Portal](https://lms4.learnshare.com/dashboard/dash.home.aspx?Z=geSEmDtE4xwuyTwg6Sx4LdU0faDk0s5hWZ6ILUmcii%2f8U1QENEJVyUzrmcgfZtbj&cid=236).
2. Select the **Student Registration** button (bottom right).
3. Create a student account.
4. On the registration form:
   - **User ID (email):** use your personal email (not your school email)
   - **E-mail:** use your **personal email address** (not your school email)
   - **Team Number:** type `FRC6498`

### Homework #2: Complete your FIRST Safety modules

1. Go to the *Safety Assignments* tab in your FIRST Safety Learning Portal.
2. Select *My Safety Learning Opportunities* from the dropdown menu.
3. Click the red button next to each assigned learning module.
4. Complete all assigned modules by the deadline (see the [team calendar](/calendar/)).
5. Need help? See the [Safety Portal Student Learner's Guide (PDF)](/files/safety-portal-student-guide.pdf).

## Castle Robotics Team Safety Manual

### Homework #3: Read the Castle Robotics Safety Manual

1. Closely read this season's [Castle Robotics Safety Manual (PDF)](/files/safety-manual.pdf).
2. All team members must read this manual.
3. Complete this homework by its due date (see the [team calendar](/calendar/)).

## Castle Robotics Safety Contract

### Homework #4: Sign and submit the Safety Contract

1. After completing Homework #1, #2, and #3, print the [Castle Robotics Safety Contract (PDF)](/files/safety-contract.pdf).
2. The contract must be signed by:
   - the student team member
   - the student's parent/guardian
3. Turn in the signed Safety Contract at the Business & Media Team's office door.
4. Complete this homework by its due date (see the [team calendar](/calendar/)).
