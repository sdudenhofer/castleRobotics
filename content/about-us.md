## Castle Robotics FRC 6498 Team

![Castle Robotics logo](images/brand/logo.png "right")

Founded in 2016, FRC Team 6498 is located at Castle High School in Newburgh, Indiana. As an award-winning team, we work to cultivate excitement, understanding, passion, and skill in STEAM through the *FIRST* Robotics program. We seek to foster skills in students through teamwork, community outreach, public speaking, critical thinking, and unique experiences while demonstrating Gracious Professionalism in all we do.

## Our Mission

To create and promote a lasting impact of science and technology for our community with a long-term goal of building a firm foundation for the next generation of STEAM.

## FIRST Mission

> The mission of *FIRST* is to inspire young people to be science and technology leaders and innovators, by engaging them in exciting mentor-based programs that build science, engineering, and technology skills, that inspire innovation, and that foster well-rounded life capabilities including self-confidence, communication, and leadership.

## FIRST Vision

> To transform our culture by creating a world where science and technology are celebrated and where young people dream of becoming science and technology leaders.

## FIRST Competitions

### The Ultimate Sport for the Mind

Under strict rules and limited time and resources, teams of high school students are challenged to build industrial-size robots to play a difficult field game in alliance with other teams, while also fundraising to meet their goals, designing a team "brand," and advancing respect and appreciation for STEM within the local community.
