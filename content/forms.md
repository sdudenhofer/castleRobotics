## Student Handbook

All students and parents must closely read the [Student Handbook 2026–27 (PDF)](/files/robotics-handbook-2026-27.pdf).

### Student Member Contract

1. Print the last page of the [Student Handbook](/files/robotics-handbook-2026-27.pdf).
2. The contract must be signed by both the student and a parent/guardian.
3. Turn in the signed contract at the Business & Media Team's office door by its due date.
